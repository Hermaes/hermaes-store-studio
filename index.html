Questions? We've Got Answers.

Is the $149 fee applied to a makeover?

Yes. If you choose one of the makeover packages, the $149 you paid for your Store Check is deducted from that package price.

Will you need my Shopify password?

No. A public-facing Store Check does not require your password. For approved implementation work, access is granted through Shopify's collaborator system.

Will you make changes without my permission?

No. You approve a written list of changes before implementation. Checkout, payments, discounts, and orders are not modified without specific written approval.

Do you guarantee increased sales?

No. We identify and address issues that may create friction for shoppers. Sales outcomes depend on many factors outside the scope of an audit or makeover.

Are the package prices final?

Published prices are starting package prices based on the stated scope. We confirm the exact work, product coverage, and final price in writing before beginning.
